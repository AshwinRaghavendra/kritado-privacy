# Kritado Privacy Policy

Effective date November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kritado handles information when you use the app on Windows, Android, or iOS.

## Summary

Kritado is designed to work offline. Kritado does not collect personal information from your use of the app.

- You can use Kritado without creating an account.
- Kritado stores your tasks and settings on your device.
- Kritado uses no in-app analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

Kritado stores your tasks, lists, notes, journal entries, checklists, attachments, completion history, preferences, and reminder settings locally. Unfinished drafts also stay on your device. On Windows, Kritado encrypts task content, journal entries, attachments, and drafts for your signed-in Windows account. Record identifiers and reminder times are stored separately for scheduling. On Android, Kritado uses the app’s private storage under Android device protection and excludes it from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. On iOS, Kritado stores your workspace under iOS file protection in its private app container and excludes it from device backups.

If you choose to take a photo, record a voice note, or import a file, Kritado uses the camera, microphone, or file picker for that action. Content you attach is stored with your workspace. Dictation uses on-device speech recognition when available.

## Notifications

Kritado schedules task reminders and focus timer alerts on your device. Android may ask you to allow notifications and scheduled alarms. On iOS, you can allow notifications in Settings → Reminders. Task reminders can show a task title. To hide it, use **Settings → Reminders → Hide task titles in reminder notifications**.

On Windows, you can allow Task Scheduler to start Kritado for a reminder after you quit. Windows stores the next time and executable path under your user account.

## Network use

Kritado works offline. If you enable OneDrive sync on Windows or Android, the app contacts Microsoft to sign in and transfers encrypted tasks, lists, journal entries, attachments, templates and portable preferences to a dedicated folder in your personal OneDrive. Microsoft handles sign-in and storage under its privacy policy. The same Microsoft account can restore synced data on another device. The app folder contains both the encrypted changes and their recovery key. Anyone with read access to the folder can recover the data. Local drafts, sign-in credentials, permission grants, and active focus timers are excluded from sync. Disconnecting stops sync on that device. Deleting an item removes it from the synced workspace. Earlier sync files in OneDrive can still contain its data. To erase the cloud workspace, first disconnect all devices, then remove its app folder from OneDrive and its recycle bin. If you open a link, your browser handles the visit. The operating system and app store have their own privacy practices.

## Data sharing

Kritado does not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers. When you choose to share a checklist, export a file, print, or add an event to a calendar, the content goes to the app, service, or location you select. The recipient or storage provider handles that copy under its own privacy practices.

Readable exports can be opened by anyone with access to them. A protected workspace backup is encrypted on your device. Restoring it requires your passphrase. Kritado does not receive or recover the passphrase. Task text exports can include detailed notes. A journal export includes private entries and their details in the date range you select. It can include dated task notes and, for HTML, embedded photos if you choose. Workspace backups can include private journal entries, notes, and attachment contents.

## Data retention and deletion

Tasks and settings stay on your device until you remove them. To delete a task and its edit history, move it to Trash, open it there, and choose **Delete permanently**.

To erase the entire workspace on Android, clear Kritado's app storage or uninstall it. On iOS, delete Kritado from the device. On Windows, quit Kritado before removing the local **%LOCALAPPDATA%\Kritado** workspace folder. These actions delete the local workspace, so export a backup first if you want to keep a copy. Copies you saved or shared remain at their destinations until you remove them there.

## Children's privacy

Kritado is a general-purpose task app. Its no-collection policy applies to all users, including children.

## Security

Kritado limits data exposure by using local app storage and working offline. Your device's security settings and the destinations you choose for exports help determine who can access your data.

## Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this policy if Kritado's features, permissions, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the policy was updated.
