# Kritado iOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains how Kritado handles information on iPhone and iPad.

## Summary

Kritado works offline and does not require an account. Kritado does not collect personal information from your use of the app or use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Kritado stores your tasks, lists, notes, journal entries, checklists, attachments, drafts, completion history, preferences, and reminder settings in the app’s private storage. The workspace uses iOS file protection and is excluded from device backups.

When you select a file or photo using the iOS picker, Kritado stores the attachment in your workspace.

## Notifications

You can allow local task reminders and focus timer alerts under **Settings → Reminders** in Kritado. Task reminders can show a task title. To hide it, enable **Hide task titles in reminder notifications**. These notifications are scheduled on your device.

## Network Use

Kritado for iPhone and iPad works offline and does not offer OneDrive sync. If you open a link, your browser handles the visit. Apple and any services you choose to use have their own privacy practices.

## Data Sharing

Kritado does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers. When you choose to share, export, print, or add an event to Calendar, the content goes to the app, service, or location you select. That destination handles its copy under its own privacy practices.

Readable exports may include private tasks, notes, journal entries, or attachments. A protected workspace backup is encrypted on your device and requires your passphrase to restore. Kritado does not receive or recover that passphrase.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your workspace stays on your device until you remove it. To permanently delete a task and its edit history, move the task to Trash and choose **Delete permanently**. To erase the whole workspace, delete Kritado from your device. Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy. Files you exported or shared remain at their destinations until you remove them there.

## Children’s Privacy

Kritado is a general-purpose task and journal app. The same no-collection policy applies to all users, including children.

## Security

Kritado limits data exposure by working offline and keeping the workspace in private app storage. Your device’s security settings and the destinations you choose for exports affect who can access your data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features, permissions, or data practices change. The effective date at the top will show when the policy was updated.
